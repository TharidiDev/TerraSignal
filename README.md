## 🌎 The Problem

Many older adults are comfortable with basic mobile phones but may find modern smartphone-based weather services difficult to access or navigate.

Small interfaces, maps, complex apps, internet requirements, and information overload can create barriers between people and important environmental information.

**TerraSignal asks a simple question:**

> *What if accessing environmental intelligence required nothing more than a missed call?*

## 💡 Our Solution

**TerraSignal** is a NASA-data-powered early-warning concept designed around **zero-learning access**.

A registered user can give a missed call from a basic phone. TerraSignal identifies the user's location, processes relevant environmental and weather data, evaluates potential weather risks, and returns a simple personalized warning through **SMS and/or automated voice**.

No smartphone.
No app.
No complicated interface.

Just a phone call and a clear signal.

## 🛰️ NASA Data

TerraSignal explores NASA Earth-observation resources such as:

* **NASA POWER** — environmental and meteorological data
* **NASA GIBS** — Earth-observation imagery and visualization resources

NASA data is incorporated into the environmental intelligence and risk-analysis layer rather than being used only as a decorative data source.

## 📡 How It Works

**Basic Phone → Missed Call → TerraSignal → NASA/Environmental Data → Risk Analysis → Personalized Alert → SMS/Voice**

The goal is to transform complex Earth-observation information into something a person can understand and act on immediately.

## 👵 Designed for Accessibility

TerraSignal is designed with older adults and low-connectivity users in mind:

* 📱 Works around basic-phone access
* 📞 Missed-call interaction
* 🔤 Simple, readable messages
* 🗣️ Optional voice delivery
* 🇱🇰 Sinhala-first communication
* 🌐 Designed with future multilingual expansion in mind

## 🧪 Validation

The project will be evaluated through:

* Historical weather-event analysis
* Comparison between predicted risk and observed conditions
* False-alarm and missed-warning analysis
* Alert delivery time
* Usability testing with representative users

## 🚀 Vision

TerraSignal begins with a simple use case in Sri Lanka:

**making environmental intelligence accessible to people who are often left behind by smartphone-first technology.**

The same architecture could eventually support other languages, regions, hazards, and communities with limited digital access.
